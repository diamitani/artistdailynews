# 19. Product Roadmap (Stages 0–6) — ArtistDailyNews.com

| Stage | Milestones | Primary Objective |
|---|---|---|
| **Stage 0: Foundation (Current)** | Next.js 16 media frontend, RSS ingestion pipeline, AI Newsroom Agent, Press Pass portal, financial calculator, Google AdSense slots, Stripe checkout. | Command instant credibility; launch with 50+ sources. |
| **Stage 1: Audience Scale (1k–10k Users)** | Automated morning email dispatch via Resend; syndication to Apple News and Flipboard; first direct sponsor deals. | Reach 10,000 active newsletter subscribers. |
| **Stage 2: Community & Badging (10k–100k Users)** | User accounts, saved bookmarks, custom topic alerts, verified artist badges, and festival media photo galleries. | Enable user accounts & personalized feeds. |
| **Stage 3: Deal Flow & Directory (100k–1M Users)** | Self-serve music sync licensing directory, record label A&R submission portal, and grant application tracker. | Institutionalize indie industry deal flow. |
| **Stage 4: Audio & Video Studio (1M–10M Users)** | Daily 5-minute AI-voiced audio briefing on Spotify/Apple Podcasts; YouTube daily news recap automation. | Multi-modal media dominance. |
| **Stage 5: Global Enterprise (10M+ Users)** | Multi-language translation (Latin America, Europe, Asia); international rights databases; global press pass network. | The Wall Street Journal of independent music. |
