# 02. Jobs To Be Done (JTBD) — ArtistDailyNews.com (ADN)

## 1. Primary Jobs

### Job 1: Daily Music Industry Intelligence & Advantage
> **When** I wake up and plan my release strategy or daily music business tasks,  
> **I want to** quickly scan a clean, categorized, 30-second summary of what just happened in the music industry (royalties, playlisting, tech, marketing trends),  
> **So that I can** make smarter career decisions, avoid bad contracts, and leverage new opportunities before my peers.

**Success Metrics:**
- Under 2 minutes to consume the daily morning briefing.
- Direct links to original sources + immediate actionable takeaways.

---

### Job 2: Official Press Pass & Credential Acquisition
> **When** a major festival (Coachella, Rolling Loud, SXSW), tour, or industry conference comes to town,  
> **I want to** apply for official press credentials and media accreditation under the verified Artist Daily News publication banner,  
> **So that I can** gain media pit access, interview artists, network with executives, and elevate my brand authority.

**Success Metrics:**
- Instant application submission with portfolio verification.
- Professional digital Media Kit generation.

---

### Job 3: Automated Audience Distribution & Monetization (For the Platform Operator)
> **When** breaking news or daily trends emerge,  
> **I want to** autonomously aggregate stories, draft newsletters, schedule social posts, and serve high-CPM programmatic & direct sponsor ads,  
> **So that I can** build an empire media brand with zero manual writing grind and generate diversified ad + subscription revenue.

**Success Metrics:**
- 100% automated ingestion and AI summarization pipeline.
- AdSense + Sponsor slots populated dynamically on all web pages.

---

## 2. Secondary & Emotional Jobs

- **Status & Belonging**: Independent artists feel they have their own "Wall Street Journal" / "Variety" that champions indie creators rather than solely mega-popstars.
- **Financial Confidence**: Tools like the streaming payout calculator and catalogue valuation estimator remove mystery from music economics.
- **Network Effects**: Getting listed on the ADN newsletter puts artists in front of managers, A&Rs, and potential collaborators.
