# 23. Legal Pack & Editorial Policy — ArtistDailyNews.com

## 1. Editorial Attribution & Fair Use
ArtistDailyNews.com operates as a news aggregation and analysis entity. All aggregated headlines link directly to original primary source publishers (Billboard, Music Ally, Digital Music News, etc.) with explicit attribution. AI-generated 30-second bullet syntheses and DIY takeaways constitute transformative analysis protected under fair use doctrines.

## 2. Press Pass Accreditation Disclaimers
Press accreditation letters issued by Artist Daily News represent legitimate editorial assignments. Final access decisions rest solely with event organizers, festival juries, and promoter PR teams.

## 3. Advertising & Sponsored Content Guidelines
All paid partner content, sponsored reviews, and affiliate partner links are clearly demarcated with `SPONSORED` or `PARTNER SPOTLIGHT` badges in compliance with FTC guidelines.
